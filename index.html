Reduces water content in the oil. The right treatment depends on how much water there is, whether it's free, emulsified or dissolved, and on the oil and the equipment.

About F&F Oil Solutions

F&F Oil Solutions provides industrial oil filtration, dehydration and reclamation services for manufacturers and equipment operators. We work with each customer to understand the oil, equipment and operating conditions before recommending a service.

Our goal is to help customers make informed decisions about oil condition and equipment maintenance.

FAQ

Questions plants ask us.

What types of oil can F&F evaluate?

Hydraulic, lubricating, gear and other industrial oils across a wide range of ISO viscosity grades, from light to heavy. Compatibility is confirmed for each oil and application, so send us the product name, viscosity grade and equipment details.

Can you treat oil contaminated with water?

Often, yes. The right method depends on how much water there is and whether it is free, emulsified or dissolved, along with the oil and the equipment. An assessment or a Karl Fischer test tells us what we're dealing with.

Can you remove metal particles?

Filtration removes suspended solid particles, including wear metals. We also recommend finding the source, since metal in the oil can point to component wear.

Can all used oil be recovered?

No. Recovery depends on viscosity, degradation, additive condition, contamination and the equipment manufacturer's requirements. Some oils are better replaced, and we'll tell you when that's the case.

What analysis do you recommend before service?

It depends on the application. Common tests are viscosity at 40 °C, particle count (ISO 4406), water by Karl Fischer, ICP for wear metals, FTIR for oxidation, and TAN.

How long does the process take?

It depends on oil volume, viscosity, temperature and contamination level. We give you an estimate once we've reviewed your application.

What information do you need for a quote?

Machine or system type, oil type or viscosity if known, approximate volume, the contamination you've observed, and your location. Existing lab reports, data sheets or photos help a lot.

Do you offer service at the customer's facility?

Yes. Our service is designed to be performed on site, coordinated with your maintenance and safety teams.

Do you have equipment for rent or demonstration?

Ask us about current availability. We'll confirm capacity, what's included, requirements and rental terms.

Does the machine need to be stopped during service?

It depends on the equipment and how it's connected. Some systems can be treated while running and others need to be stopped. We define this with your team during planning.

How do you determine whether oil can be reused?

With the assessment and lab analysis, compared against the application and the equipment manufacturer's specifications. We don't promise reuse before we know the oil's condition.

What happens to used filters and waste?

Handling of used filters and waste is agreed with you before the work starts, in line with your plant's procedures.
